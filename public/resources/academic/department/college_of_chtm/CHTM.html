College of Hospitality and Tourism Management

Shaping global hospitality leaders for a dynamic future

The College of Hospitality and Tourism Management at St. John Paul II College of Davao is committed to equipping students with the skills and knowledge necessary to excel in the dynamic and fast-paced hospitality and tourism industry. With a strong emphasis on professionalism, innovation, and cultural awareness, our programs are designed to prepare future leaders who will shape the global tourism and hospitality landscape.

Through a blend of rigorous academic training and hands-on practical experiences, our students gain comprehensive expertise in areas such as hotel management, culinary arts, event planning, and sustainable tourism. We take pride in nurturing individuals who not only excel in their careers but also embody values of service, leadership, and integrity.
