Welcome to Our Property Custodian

At John Paul College of Davao, our Property Custodian Office is passionately committed to ensuring the efficient management and safeguarding of school properties and resources. We provide tailored support to empower our community in realizing the importance of responsible property stewardship. Our dedicated team of experienced custodians is here to assist in maintaining and optimizing the use of our facilities, ensuring a conducive environment for learning and growth.

"Caring for our campus and resources for a brighter tomorrow."
"Empowering our community to cherish and maintain our shared spaces through responsible stewardship and support."
